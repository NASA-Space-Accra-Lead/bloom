3. Health Information

Information about your physical or mental health or condition is “special personal data” under the Act, and receives extra protection. If you describe symptoms, pain, medical history or other health details in the booking form, in messages, or during your visit, we treat it as special personal data.

By ticking the consent box on our booking form, you agree that we may process the information you provide, including any health information you choose to include in your notes, to arrange and prepare for your appointment. You do not have to include health details in the form. You can add them when you speak to us instead. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 9).

We use health information only for providing and managing your care and for the legal and record-keeping duties that apply to dental and wellness providers. Access is limited to staff who need it.

4. How And Why We Use Your Information

  • Booking And Appointments: to receive your request, confirm it by SMS or WhatsApp, reschedule if needed, and prepare for your visit.
  • Providing Care And Services: to deliver dental, wellness and beauty services, including keeping appropriate records.
  • Communication: to answer questions and send service-related messages such as reminders and follow-up.
  • Payments: to record and reconcile payments, including Mobile Money and installment plans.
  • Legal And Regulatory Duties: to meet requirements of the Ghana Dental Council, HeFRA, tax and other laws.
  • Running And Improving The Studio: to manage operations and service quality.

We rely on your consent (especially for health information), on the need to take steps at your request to provide a service (contract), on our legal obligations, and, for routine operations, on our legitimate interests in running the studio, where these do not override your rights. We use your information only for the purposes above. If we want to use it for a new purpose, we will tell you first and, where required, ask for your consent.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not make decisions about you using fully automated processing.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We share personal information only where needed and only with:

  • Our staff and partner practitioners who are involved in your care, bound by confidentiality.
  • Service providers (data processors) that act on our instructions, including the cloud database provider that stores booking records (Supabase), our website hosting provider, and messaging services such as WhatsApp and SMS that we use to contact you.
  • Payment providers, such as MTN Mobile Money and Vodafone Cash, if you pay through them. They handle your payment details under their own policies.
  • Professionals you are referred to, such as a specialist or emergency provider, with your knowledge.
  • Regulators, courts and law enforcement where the law requires or permits it.

Our website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is sent to Google when you visit. We require our processors to protect your information and to use it only for the services they provide to us.

6. Transfers Outside Ghana

Some of our providers store or process data on servers outside Ghana. Our booking database is hosted by Supabase in EU (Frankfurt or Ireland). Where your information is transferred abroad, we take steps to ensure it receives a level of protection consistent with the Act, including contractual safeguards with the provider, and we transfer it only with your consent or where the Act otherwise permits.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and professional record-keeping duties.

  • Booking requests that do not lead to a visit: class="todo"> 12 Months
  • Patient and Treatment records: 12 Months
  • Payment and Financial records: 12 Months

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure and misuse. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) for our website, access restricted to authorised staff, and a managed cloud database. No online system is completely secure, so please avoid sending information you consider highly sensitive through the booking form. You can tell us in person or by phone instead.

9. Your Rights

Under the Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about why and how we process your information (this policy).
  • Access the personal information we hold about you and ask for a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Request deletion or destruction of information we are no longer entitled to keep.
  • Object to or restrict processing that causes you unwarranted damage or distress.
  • Object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before you withdrew, and it may mean we can no longer provide some services.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission (see section 15).

To exercise any right, contact us using the details in section 15. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within a reasonable time and as the Act requires. Some records must be kept by law, in which case we will explain what we can and cannot delete.

10. Children

We welcome patients of all ages. Appointments for a child should be booked by a parent or legal guardian, who consents on the child’s behalf. We process children’s information only with that consent and only to provide their care.

11. Marketing

We will not send you promotional messages unless you have agreed to receive them. Contact details you give us to book an appointment are used for service messages. You can opt out of any marketing at any time by telling us or using the opt-out in the message.

12. Cookies And Website Technology

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting and security systems may automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, which we use only to keep the site running and secure. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Links to third-party services (for example, WhatsApp) take you to sites with their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

13. Data Breaches

If a security breach affects your personal information, we will take steps to contain it and, as the Act requires, notify the Data Protection Commission and the people affected as soon as reasonably practicable.

14. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change to how we use your information, we will tell you.
