Frequently asked questions

What should I expect at my first visit?

We’ll talk through your goals and concerns, carry out an examination, and explain your options before any treatment. Please arrive a few minutes early.

How soon will my appointment be confirmed?

After you submit the form, our team confirms by SMS or WhatsApp using the number you provide.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We welcome patients of all ages, from toddlers to grandparents.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Choose “Emergency Dental Care Referral” in the booking form or call us directly, and we’ll advise you on the next step.

How can I pay?

We accept MTN Mobile Money and Vodafone Cash, and offer flexible payment plans for larger treatments.
